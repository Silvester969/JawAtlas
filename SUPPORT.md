# Getting help

- **How do I use a feature?** Ask in [Discussions → Q&A](https://github.com/Silvester969/JawAtlas/discussions).
- **Something is broken.** Open a [bug report](https://github.com/Silvester969/JawAtlas/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **I have an idea.** Open a [feature request](https://github.com/Silvester969/JawAtlas/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **I want to build a teaching case.** Open a [teaching case proposal](https://github.com/Silvester969/JawAtlas/issues/new?template=teaching_case.yml).
- **Private matters.** Email jawatlas@icloud.com.

JawAtlas is an education and training aid. It is not a medical device, and we cannot answer questions about diagnosing or treating a specific patient.
